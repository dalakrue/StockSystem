## Durable findings

1. The TP-continuation re-entry partner is the round's one
   transferable family — positive in Nov, Dec, Jan, Apr;
   negative only in March (exhausted-move re-buys) and on
   February's raw control (pays there only on the suppressed
   base). Where it works, it adds trades at the incumbent's
   own checks with no frequency cost and improves worst-5
   loss windows (Nov -179.28 -> -111.80).
2. The volatility-shock veto works only in Nov/Dec (2-for-5
   as a screen across the round, incl. Feb +4.09 as C10's
   second ingredient) — it pays only where a month's
   high-vol_z picks are genuinely waste; fit per month,
   never port. Jan -11.81 and Apr -2.68 with foreign fits.
3. Score-gating is what makes added volume work: ungated
   dual-picks dilute everywhere (Apr -10.6 to -22.6); the
   eq-score gate turns the same design positive (Jan near-
   miss stack, Apr winner).
4. M15/M30 confirmation vetoes are dead in all 7 months
   (catastrophic Dec -50.66/-67.08, Feb, Apr -40.98/-53.15;
   useless elsewhere) — do not retest as a family.
5. Win-by-removal always dies on frequency: February's
   best-ever quality (leftover 92.80) still fails the >=2/day
   floors and the MC median clause. Screen volume impact
   before quality.
6. LCT q05 is hostage to weak early discovery blocks (Jan);
   partners adding trades in weak blocks defend it. And a
   very high incumbent AUDIT leftover (Jan 67.13) makes the
   materiality audit clause bind even when audit net rises.
7. Construction lessons: re-entry builders must use
   full-interval conflict checking (Nov's first pass caught
   2 overlapping EURNZD legs) and the partner check must be
   strictly after the base trade's own check AND its exit
   (Mar's exit_time-convention trap); TREND-stamped FB rows
   need side resolution by the incumbent's mom24 rule before
   any transform (Dec).
