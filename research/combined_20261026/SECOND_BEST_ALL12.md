## Selection rule (applied uniformly)
1. A JUDGED candidate (reached refit / audit) outranks all.
2. Else a candidate that passed FIT+HOLDOUT outranks FIT-only.
3. Else the FIT-best of the stronger track — a challenger that
   cleared the FIT gate outranks one stopped at it; between two
   stopped at the same gate, the smallest shortfall vs that
   gate wins. The A3/BRIEF17 incarnation of a construct is
   preferred; the runner-up is listed where near-tied.
For challengers never refit, "discovery" = FIT segment stats
(n / leftover / DD) plus full-discovery leftover where the
record gives it (Track B); no audit exists for them.
