Super Quick / Field 3 Upgrade — 2026-08-24

- Super Quick no longer falls through to the slow generic instant-run queue when
  the bounded Field 3 engine reports an error. The click now fails safely,
  clears stale cooperative locks, and preserves the last valid result.
- Finder backfill now runs after the Field 3 ranking/evidence frames are staged.
  Historical regime age, starting price, completed-run count, and average
  duration are calculated with vectorized pandas operations instead of a
  Python loop for every candle.
- A duplicate compact load progress bar is shown directly beneath the top
  “Load All Selected (Top 30)” action and is updated through completion.
- Middle Standard CSV/XLSX and Finder CSV/XLSX exports now use the same
  canonical Field 3 order. SL is immediately after Symbol, followed by
  Strategy Decision and Weighted Risk Decision.
- Weighted Risk block selection remains capped at four per calendar hour and
  selects the largest Risk_Score values first. SL exits remain capped at six per
  hour and select the largest Weighted_Risk_Score values first.
- Finder presets include Custom, Last 365 Days, Last 2 Years, and All Loaded
  History; preset ranges are calculated from the actual loaded history bounds.
