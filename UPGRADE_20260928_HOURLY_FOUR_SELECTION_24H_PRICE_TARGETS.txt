UPGRADE: Middle Regime Ranking + Historical Finder
Date: 2026-09-28
Version: hourly-four-selector-v1-20260928

WHAT CHANGED
============
1. EXACTLY FOUR PUBLISHED OPPORTUNITIES PER COMPLETED HOUR
   - When 4+ symbols are available, the public Strategy Decision layer selects
     exactly 4 symbols per completed hourly snapshot.
   - Genuine S1-S110 entry-condition hits are ranked first.
   - If fewer than 4 genuine hits exist, the remaining slots are filled from
     the strongest non-hit situations across the other symbols.
   - Filled rows are explicitly labelled RANK-FILL. The underlying S1-S110
     audit remains truthful; a rank-fill is NOT fabricated as an S1-S110 hit.
   - Unselected rows show Strategy Decision = None.
   - Selected rows receive rank #1/4 ... #4/4 and the selected-first table order.
   - The same four-row decision publication is applied to the live Middle
     Standard Regime Ranking and the Historical Finder.

2. MORE ENTRY OPPORTUNITIES WITHOUT REWRITING S1-S110 TRUTH
   - Existing S1-S110 trigger definitions remain intact.
   - The previously requested S1-S92 30% relaxation remains in the shared
     strategy audit.
   - A causal Near Entry Score is used to compare non-hit symbols and fill
     unused hourly slots.
   - Selection scoring considers actual strategy hits, number of hits, best
     strategy score, near-entry quality, regime alignment, price TP/SL ratio,
     and volatility-normalized TP potential.

3. DYNAMIC 24-HOUR PRICE TP/SL
   - Suggested TP / Suggested SL are now actual MARKET PRICES, not pips.
   - Target distance uses completed-candle information only: ATR, historical
     24-hour realized range, prior-range P85/P70 statistics, volatility move,
     directional room, trend strength, and momentum.
   - A causal upper envelope limits the target to a volatility-aware maximum-
     likely range instead of allowing a single old spike to dominate forever.
   - SL uses recent structure plus ATR-based floor/cap and is placed on the
     correct side of price for BUY/SELL.
   - Additional visible/export fields include:
       Suggested TP Price
       Suggested SL Price
       TP Price Display
       SL Price Display
       24H Historical Range
       24H Range P85
       24H Volatility Move
       24H TP Distance
       24H TP Upper Envelope
       24H SL Distance
       TP Estimate Basis
       TP:SL Price Ratio
       TP Within 24H

4. UI / FINDER INTEGRATION
   - Strategy Decision is shown beside Symbol in the primary ranking layout.
   - Four selected symbols are promoted to the top of each hourly group.
   - Finder preview and exports retain the new four-slot fields and price-based
     target fields.
   - Finder status text identifies the S1-S110 + hourly four-symbol layer.

DESIGN NOTE
===========
The request to make every hour show four opportunities is implemented as a
separate hourly portfolio-selection layer. It does not falsify the original
strategy audit by changing False S1-S110 conditions to True. This preserves
backtest/audit traceability while still guaranteeing four published candidates
when the loaded universe contains at least four symbols.
